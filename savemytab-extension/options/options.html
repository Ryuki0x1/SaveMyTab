Per-Tab Custom Controls

Fine-grained control over individual tabs directly from the extension popup or context menu:

⏱ Fixed Inactivity Timer per Tab
Open the SaveMyTab popup on any active tab and pick a fixed duration (1m, 5m, 10m, 30m, 1h, or custom minutes). That specific tab will follow its own timer instead of the global default.
🛡 Per-Tab Suspension Lock
Toggle "Exclude this tab" in the popup or right-click the page and select "Toggle Exclude this tab from suspension". This prevents that individual tab from ever being unloaded, even if you never pin it.
